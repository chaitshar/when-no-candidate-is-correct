# Prior-art recheck (full abstracts and arXiv HTML, 2026-11-03)

Method: curl of arxiv.org/abs/<id> (titles, authors, dates, version lists, abstracts, comments and journal-ref fields) and arxiv.org/html/<id> for the full text of 14 papers, searched for both-wrong, all-incorrect, no-correct, absent, randomized, mediation, future-work and venue wording. Layers: L1 = LLM judge choosing a wrong candidate from a pool with no correct answer, vs judging the same candidates alone; L2 = debate where all debaters are wrong; L3 = randomized valid/invalid critiques with causal mediation; T = pairwise tournaments on pools with no correct candidate.

No abs page carries a journal-ref or a comment naming an accepted venue, so every citation file is "arXiv preprint". Venues quoted in the scoop files that do not appear on the arXiv pages: NeurIPS 2026 (2611.00507, 2606.02646), EMNLP 2025 Findings (2509.14034), ICSE 2027 (2610.29147). These remain unverified.

## Result

- REVERSED: 2407.04622 (Kenton et al.), for the claim that it flags both-wrong debate as future work. The U3 kill cites this claim, so U3 needs re-review. The B_venues quote "could both choose to argue for the incorrect answers" is also wrong.
- Open: no arXiv paper among the 20 ids shows L1, L2, L3 or T as a completed design. 2402.01349 (section 4.2) comes closest on L1 and 2608.25937 excludes the all-wrong regime explicitly.
- Not fetched: 2608.17270v2 (404). The scoop reference to a v2 was not real.
- Citation files: 17 new, in evidence/citations/. Skipped as duplicates of existing files for the same papers: kenton2024scalable.json (2407.04622), jeong2025comparative.json (2406.12319), wang2025llms.json (2402.01349).
