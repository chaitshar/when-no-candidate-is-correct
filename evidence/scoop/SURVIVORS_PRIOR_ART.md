A NeurIPS 2026 poster, "Selected-Tail Reliability in Verifier-Guided Best-of-N Inference" (neurips.cc/virtual/2026/poster/155878), appeared in search; I found no arXiv id and did not open the page, so it is unverified and not tabled. It is worth checking: its subject (reliability of the selected output after optimizing over noisy verifier scores) is the closest theory to R2.

Overall R2: not found as an empirical result. The pieces exist separately (selection-induced false acceptance in 2510.04087 and the poster; same-model rubber-stamping in 2611.00507; weak self-assessment of own false positives in 2607.11022), but none measures the selector's pick vs other wrong candidates under isolated same-model verification in all-wrong pools. Verdict: novel in combination; frame as an instance of selection-induced optimism and cite these.
