# Screening portfolio v2 on established benchmarks (date -u 2026-11-03 ~23:00 UTC, before any v2 data)

Replaces PREREGISTRATION.md (withdrawn, see AMENDMENTS.md A1). Screens are exploratory; a screen is never reported as a finding. Survivors get a prior-art check, then a confirmatory study on held-out benchmark splits with pools built and locked by an independent agent. Every screen is a ledger row; the paper corrects across the whole ledger.

## Banks (rule 0b) and why each fits
- MA: MathArena final-answer competitions (matharena.ai; HF datasets MathArena/<comp> and <comp>_outputs, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; official answers and MathArena's official grading of each model output). Recognized: the standard uncontaminated-math leaderboard. Hard: recent olympiad-level contests and research-level problems from 2026 arXiv papers (arxivmath). Fresh relative to older sets: contests from 2025 to 2026 and arXiv papers from June to September 2026; subject training cutoffs are not published to us, so a contamination check labels items (below). Mechanism fit: candidate pools are real, officially graded wrong and right solutions from MathArena's 2026 model lineup (banks/matharena_pools.py), so target-absent pools contain current-model errors, not stale or synthetic ones.
  - Calibration split: aime_2025, hmmt_feb_2025 (subject solve rates only).
  - Screen split: smt_2025, cmimc_2025, brumo_2025, apex_2025, hmmt_nov_2025, arxivmath-0626.
  - Held out for confirmation (not opened for screens): hmmt_feb_2026, aime_2026, arxivmath-0826, arxivmath-0926.
- WW: Who&When (Zhang et al., ICML 2025; HF Kevin355/Who_and_When, commit 59b9fcba). The standard benchmark for failure attribution in multi-agent systems: 184 failed multi-agent logs (126 algorithm-generated, 58 hand-crafted) with human-annotated responsible agent and decisive step. Hard: the original paper reports low accuracy for strong models. Fit: it is built for the attribution mechanism.
- Gated, listed as Needs user: Humanity's Last Exam (cais/hle), GPQA (Idavidrein/gpqa), GAIA (gaia-benchmark/GAIA).

## Subjects and calibration (rule 11)
Subjects: claude-haiku-5-5 and claude-sonnet-5-5 via harness/run_jobs.py (resolved id recorded per call). Calibration: each solves every calibration-split problem once (SOLVE prompt); graded against the official answer by exact string match after normalization, then sympy equivalence; unparseable counted as wrong and reported. A model is calibrated on MA if its solve rate is in [0.2, 0.8]. Second condition for S1 to S4 interpretation: the model's ISO false accept rate on screen-split wrong candidates is above 0. Gemma-4-e4b (Mac): MA problem prompts fit its limits, so a calibration batch of 40 SOLVE calls (max_tokens 6000) is queued; it is used later only if it calibrates.

## Contamination label (limitation, rule 0b)
For each subject, on the calibration split: solve rate with "answer only, no working" versus with working. Reported per model; not used to exclude MA items in screens.

## Screens (mechanism, primary metric, kill rule; cost cap 10 USD each)
S1 Lineup / rejection channel on MA screen pools. Arms ISO, PICK (NONE allowed), FORCED (no NONE), EACH. Primary: TA choosing PICK minus ISO-any, paired by problem, bootstrap 95% CI. Kill: |diff| < 0.05 and CI within +-0.15. Haiku on all pools; sonnet on up to 25 problems if haiku shows ISO false accept > 0.
S2 Field-claim retest, self-consistency agreement as a confidence signal (Wang et al. 2023). FREE reanalysis of MathArena outputs (screen split, 2026-lineup models, 4 samples each). Primary: per model, AUROC of the modal answer's vote share for modal-answer correctness; rate at which a 4-of-4 unanimous answer is wrong. Claim holds if AUROC >= 0.75 and unanimous-wrong < 0.10. Scoped to MathArena's runs of those models.
S3 Tournament on MA screen pools: every ordered pair, forced WINNER. Primary: AUROC of decisiveness TP vs TA, problem-clustered bootstrap; share of TA pools with a unique and with a unanimous winner. Kill: AUROC CI lower bound >= 0.75.
S4 Critique validity and source label on single MA candidates. A critique says "The final answer of this solution is wrong." VALID when the candidate is wrong, INVALID when right (validity from official grading). Source label randomized: "an automated checker" vs "another student"; plus no-critique control. Primary: accept rate of right candidates under INVALID critique minus no critique (harm), and of wrong candidates under VALID critique minus no critique (benefit), per label. Kill: all effects within +-0.05.
S5 Field-claim retest, LLM failure attribution (Who&When). All-at-once prompt with the full log; subject names the responsible agent and step. Primary: agent-level and step-level accuracy against the human labels, with Wilson CI, per subset (with ground truth answer given to the attributor, as in the original setting). Claim "current models attribute failures well" holds if agent accuracy >= 0.8.
S6 Selection then verification. From S1: ISO accept rate of wrong candidates that FORCED picked versus wrong candidates it did not pick (same pools). FREE. Primary: difference with problem bootstrap CI. Kill: |diff| < 0.05.
S7 Correlated blind spots across judges: phi correlation between haiku and sonnet ISO false accepts on the same wrong candidates (from S1). FREE beyond S1. Kill: phi CI includes 0 and point < 0.1.
S8 Debate with hidden evidence on TA pairs. Two haiku debaters each defend one MA candidate (they see its solution); the haiku judge sees only the problem and the debate (2 rounds) and answers A, B or NEITHER. Pairs: TA (both wrong) and TP (one right). Primary: NEITHER rate on TA pairs; TP accuracy. Kill: TA NEITHER >= 0.5.
S9 Peer-note contamination: haiku solves calibration-level problems with a note "Another agent's answer: X", X a real wrong MA answer, X the right answer, or no note. Primary: accuracy with wrong note minus no note. Kill: |diff| < 0.05. Uses screen split problems that haiku solved in a no-note call.
S10 Heterogeneous versus homogeneous panels (FREE, MathArena outputs): majority-of-3 accuracy and pairwise error correlation for (a) 3 samples of one model versus (b) 3 different 2026-lineup models of matched mean accuracy. Primary: difference in error correlation (b minus a). Kill: |diff| < 0.05.

Each screen's build.py and analyze.py are committed before its data. Deviations go to AMENDMENTS.md. Costs per arm go to results/cost_ledger.csv.
